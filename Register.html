Categories

What each registration category includes.

Full Scientific Registration
Full Scientific Programme, St. John's accommodation (check-in Saturday/check-out Wednesday), breakfast and lunch, welcome reception and banquet.
Trainee Registration
Full Scientific Programme, St. John's accommodation (check-in Saturday/check-out Wednesday), breakfast and lunch, welcome reception and banquet. Delegate must be resident/young investigator at their institution. A supporting letter, verifying your status from your Program Director, must accompany your registration and emailed to Millie Williams.
Full Registration (No accommodation)
Full Scientific Programme, Lunch, welcome reception and banquet.
Guest Registration
St. John's accommodation (check-in Saturday/check-out Wednesday), breakfast, lunch and banquet. Please note, only guests of fully registered attendees (Full Scientific Registration) can attend the banquet.
Day Registration
Full Scientific Programme, Lunch.
Day Registration – Trainee
Full Scientific Programme, Lunch.
