Programme

5-day Research Meeting

Sunday 26 to Thursday 30 July 2027 at the Boston Marriot, Boston, MA.

Highlights

The Scientific Programme includes fundamental research and translational themes that focus on: Understanding scleroderma pathogenesis, Advanced cell-based therapies, (CAR-T, Dendritic, B and T regs, Myeloid, NK), Interstitial pulmonary disease, Gastrointestinal disease, Organoids and self-assembled tissues, B cell and autoantibodies, Fibroblast heterogeneity and sub-sets, Regeneration repair and metabolism, Mechanisms of pulmonary vascular disease, Advances in Platform Technologies and Omics, Cancer associated inflammation and stroma, Targeting the fibroblast/myofibroblast and Young Investigator session (abstract presentations)
