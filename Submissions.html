Submissions

Submit an Abstract

Attendees are invited to submit abstracts to the International Workshop on Scleroderma Research in either basic or clinical areas related to systemic sclerosis.

Submit Abstracts to: Millie.Williams@ucl.ac.uk

Submission Opens:

Feb. 14, 2025

Submission Closes:

June 15, 2025

Acceptance Notifications:

June 30, 2025

Abstract Guidelines

  • Abstracts should report original investigations; case reports will not be accepted.
  • Abstracts will be reviewed by the Abstract Selection Committee.
  • Abstract author will receive an email notification of abstract acceptance (oral and poster presentations) at the end of June.
  • For oral presentations, the abstract author will be asked to give a 10 minute presentation (7-minute talk followed by 3 minutes for Q&A).
  • Poster size and instructions will be included in the acceptance email (and can be found below).
  • Please provide the name of presenter and address for correspondence (including affiliation, email and contact number) in the email.
  • Two workshop travel awards are available to fellows and junior faculty with the highest ranked abstracts. See Workshop Travel Awards for Young Investigators below.

Example

STUDYING THE PATHOGENEIS OF SYSTEMIC SCLEROSIS

A.B. James, C. Bloggs, D. Another & E.F. Final

Institution/University, Department/Division, Centre/Laboratory, Address, State/County, City, Area/Post code, Country

Background:

Systemic Sclerosis is an autoimmune connective tissue disease characterised by vasculopathy, prolonged inflammation and extensive tissue remodelling leading to excessive scarring and fibrosis.

Materials and methods:

Results:

Conclusions:

Format

  • 1 page, A4 size.
  • Must be submitted in English.
  • Must not exceed 500 words (text in background to conclusions). See format example below.
  • Word count does not include: title, author name, author affiliations, acknowledgments (including working group) or abstract number.
  • Title must not exceed 25 words and should be in UPPER CASE.
  • Must be send in a Microsoft Word document; Text should be Arial 11-point font only (please type in Arial do not convert afterwards).
  • Underline contributing author.

Oral and Poster Presentations

Notification confirming abstract acceptance (oral and poster presentations) will be sent end of June.

Oral Presentation

Abstract author will be invited to give a 10-minute presentation during the meeting.

7-minute talk, followed by 3 minutes for questions.

Travel Awards

Travel Awards for Young Investigators

Two types of travel awards are available to attend the International Workshop on Scleroderma Research. Please indicate on your abstract submission which award you wish to be considered for.

A supporting letter, verifying status from your programme director, must be sent to Millie.Williams@ucl.ac.uk before your application is confirmed

$1,500 USD

towards Registration and Travel

Award A

SCTC Workshop Travel Awards

Five (5) awards will be given to eligible applicants with highly scored abstracts for participating in the 19th International Workshop on Scleroderma Research in Cambridge, England (July 2025).

Eligible applicants are:

  • Postgraduate and medical students
  • Clinical and basic science research fellows
  • Early career investigators

Additional eligibility criteria:

  1. Applicant must be the first author and the presenting author of a highly scored abstract.
  2. Only one author per abstract may be considered for the award.
  3. An individual institution is allowed only one awardee.

$7,500 USD

Awards will be disbursed by the SCTC with $2,500 given prior to the International Workshop on Scleroderma Research meeting and $5,000 prior to the American College of Rheumatology Annual Convergence Meeting in 2025.

Award B

Kao Family Foundation: SCTC Young Investigator Travel Awards

Up to Ten (10) awards will be given to eligible applicants with highly scored abstracts for participating in the 19th International Workshop on Scleroderma Research in Cambridge, England (July 2025) and the American College of Rheumatology Annual Convergence Meeting in Chicago, USA (October 2025).

Eligible applicants are:

  • Clinical Fellows
  • Junior faculty that are in the 1st or 2nd year of practice at the time of application
  • PhD students
  • Postdoctoral researchers who are in the 1st or 2nd year of research since receiving their PhD at the time of application.

Additional eligibility criteria:

  1. Applicant must be the first author and the presenting author of a highly scored abstract.
  2. Only one author per abstract may be considered for the award.
  3. An individual institution is allowed only one awardee.
  4. Applicants are only able to be selected for a Kao Family–SCTC Travel Award once.
