Venue

George Sherman Union

775 Commonwealth Ave, Boston, MA 02215

The George Sherman Union (GSU) is Boston University's central student hub, located at 775 Commonwealth Avenue. Opened in 1963, the building serves as a gathering place for students, with dining options, study and lounge spaces, student services, and venues for events and performances. Its central location next to Mugar Memorial Library makes it one of the busiest and most recognizable buildings on BU's Charles River Campus.

Transportation

The George Sherman Union is easily accessible via the MBTA Green Line B branch at Boston University Central. Driving is possible, but parking on and around campus is limited, so public transportation is strongly recommended.

Things to do in Boston

Boston is a beautiful city to explore, rich with history, culture, and stunning waterfront views. Fenway Park, the Charles River Esplanade, and Harvard Square are just a few local favorites, with plenty of museums, historic sites, neighborhoods, and restaurants to discover throughout the city.

Fenway

Catch a Red Sox game at iconic Fenway Park and experience one of Boston's favorite traditions! If you're still up for some fun after the game, head over to Lucky Strike for drinks, pool, or a round of bowling.

Newbury Street

Get your retail therapy in by shopping along Newbury Street, then grab dinner at one of the many great restaurants nearby. Just a short walk away is Owl's Nest, a beer garden serving local Boston brews right on the Esplanade. It's a beautiful spot to kick back, relax, and catch the sunset.

Harvard Square

Spend the evening exploring Harvard Square. Take a stroll through Harvard Yard and grab a burrito at a local favorite, Felipe's. Satisfy your sweet tooth with some ice cream at BerryLine and walk along the Charles River, which is just steps away. To cap off the night, catch a concert at The Sinclair or Club Passim.

North End

Explore Boston's historic North End with a nice Italian dinner at Euno or a slice of Regina's famous pizza, then grab a cannoli from Bova's Bakery. Afterward, head out on a Ghosts & Gravestones tour to hear some of the creepier stories and legends from Boston's past.
