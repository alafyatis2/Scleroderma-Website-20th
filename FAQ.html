FAQ

Registration

Can I register onsite?

No. There will be no on-site registration facilities. See Registration for registration fees and further information.

What does the registration fee include?

Registration includes access to the scientific programme, accommodation and more. See Registration for categories and further information.

Can I register first without payment?

No. Your registration to the workshop will only be confirmed when full payment is received. For registration categories, fees and payment methods, see Registration.

Accommodation

Are children allowed to stay on-site at St. Johns College?

No. Children under the age of 16 cannot be accommodated in the College.

Do all rooms come with shared facilities?

No. St. John's College does provide single ensuite rooms in addition to shared rooms/facilities.

Abstract Submission

Can I submit an abstract without registration?

No. You will receive confirmation of your abstract submission. Notification of your abstract acceptance to the workshop, with abstract type (poster/presentation), will be sent at the end of June.

Will a printing service/ facilities be available on site for abstract posters?

No. There will be no on-site poster printing service available.

Does the workshop offer support for early career researchers to attend the workshop?

Yes. Trainees (Fellows/PhD candidates) AND individuals within 3 years of their first faculty appointment are eligible to apply for a travel award.

Accreditation and Workshop Evaluation

Will delegates receive a Certificate of Participation?

Yes. As in previous years, CPD accreditation will be from the Federation of the Royal Colleges of Physicians of the United Kingdom for the 19th International Workshop on Scleroderma Research. A Certificate of Participation will be available post-workshop.

Can I give feedback/evaluation at the end of the workshop?

Yes. You will receive an email link to complete a survey at the end of the workshop.
